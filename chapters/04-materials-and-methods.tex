\chapter{Materials and Methods}\label{ch:methods}

\begin{guidance}
Describe, in enough detail for another person to reproduce your work, the
\emph{materials} (data, participants, equipment, software) and the \emph{methods}
(techniques, algorithms, experimental design, evaluation) used to achieve the objectives.
Justify every choice. Explain how the data were obtained and prepared, which
techniques were used and why, how the experiments were run (hardware and software
configuration) and how the results were evaluated. If your work involves people or
personal data, the ethical and legal section is mandatory.
\end{guidance}
