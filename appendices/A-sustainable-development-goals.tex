\chapter{Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals}\label{app:sdg}

\begin{guidance}
Several Spanish universities ask students to reflect on how their thesis relates to the
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) of the United Nations 2030 Agenda. Mark the degree of relationship of your work
with each goal by changing the last argument of each \texttt{\textbackslash sdg} command
to \texttt{H} (high), \texttt{M} (medium), \texttt{L} (low) or \texttt{N} (not
applicable), and then justify the most relevant ones. If your university does not ask for
it, remove this appendix from \texttt{main.tex}.
\end{guidance}
